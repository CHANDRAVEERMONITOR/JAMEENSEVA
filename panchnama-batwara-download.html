Bihar Land Survey: Kishtwar aur Khanapuri Special Documentation

Bihar Survey 2026 mein apne naam se khata khulwane ke liye "Sahmatí Panchnama Batwara" sabse bada kanooni saboot hai. Niche iske mahatvapurn points diye gaye hain:

1. Stamp Paper aur Legal Validity

Hamesha isey ₹100 ke Non-Judicial Stamp Paper par hi likhein. Isse ise legal validity milti hai aur Circle Office (CO) mein mutation asaan ho jata hai.

2. Hissedaron (Parties) ki Jankari

Blog Tip: Aadhar Number zaroor dalein, isse survey ke waqt identity verify turant hoti hai.

Har hissedar ka naam, pita ka naam, umr aur sahi pata hona anivarya hai.

3. Sampatti aur Chauhaddi ka Vivran

Mauza, Thana No, Khata, Khesra aur Rakwa ka pura table hona chahiye. Har hisse ki Chauhaddi (North, South, East, West) saaf likhein taaki kabza vivad na ho.

4. Vanshavali (Family Tree) Connection

Batwara mein ek Vanshvriksh hona zaroori hai. Ye sabit karta hai ki zameen aapko purvajon se uttaradhikar (inheritance) mein mili hai.

5. Sarpanch aur Gram Kachahari ki Mohar

Local validation ke liye Sarpanch ka hastakshar aur mohar document ko 100% prabhavshali banata hai.
