Muzaffarpur District Map & Block Records

Muzaffarpur ke sabhi 16 blocks ka digital record JameenSeva.in par uplabdha hai. Niche har block ki jankari thoda detail mein di gayi hai:

1. Mushahari (Town) & Kanti: Industrial & City Survey

Mushahari block (Muzaffarpur shehar) mein zameen ki registry ke liye Circle Rate (MVR) kafi tezi se badh raha hai. Kanti area mein industrial development ki wajah se purane Khatiyan aur Map ki digital copy ki mang sabse zyada hai.

2. Motipur & Sahebganj: Agriculture Land Records

Motipur block ke kisan bhaiyo ko LPC (Land Possession Certificate) ki jankari hona anivarya hai. Yahan naya survey shuru ho chuka hai, isliye apne Register 2 ka milaan jald kar lein taaki survey mein koi galti na ho.

3. Saraiya & Paroo: Mutation & Jamabandi Updates

Saraiya aur Paroo mein agar aapka Dakhil-Kharij (Mutation) pending hai, toh portal par status check karein. In blocks mein purane Panji-II ke digital records mein sudhaar (Parimarjan) ka kaam bhi tezi se chal raha hai.

4. Minapur & Bochahan: Survey Status 2026

Bochahan aur Minapur blocks mein survey ke dauran Vashavali (Family Tree) ka sahi hona bahut zaroori hai. Bina vashavali ke aapka naya khatiyan banne mein mushkil aa sakti hai.

5. Sakra & Dholi (Muraul): Proper Land Measurement

Sakra block mein zameen ki naapi (Measurement) ko lekar aksar vivad hota hai. Yahan e-Mapi ki suvidha ka upyog karein aur apna Original Map hamari site se order karein.

Anya Blocks (Aurai, Katra, Gaighat, Bandra, Marwan)

Muzaffarpur ke baaki blocks jaise Aurai, Katra, aur Gaighat mein flood-affected area hone ke karan zameen ke records kafi purane ho chuke hain. In sabhi jagaho ke liye CS/RS Khatiyan ki digital copy ab online mangwayi ja sakti hai.

Schedule Batwara: Muzaffarpur Ke Niyam

Muzaffarpur mein aapsi batwara (Panchnama) karte waqt dhyan rakhein ki Stamp Paper par sabhi hissedaro (Fariken) ke hastakshar anivarya hain. JameenSeva.in aapko ghar baithe legal draft taiyar karke deti hai taaki aapka batwara kanooni taur par mazboot rahe.

Muzaffarpur Land Record FAQs

1. Muzaffarpur ka original khatiyan kitne din mein milega?
Digital copy 24-48 ghante mein aur physical original copy 6-8 din mein aapke address par deliver ho jayegi.

2. Kya Muzaffarpur mein online rasiid kat rahi hai?
Ji haan, agar aapka Jamabandi update hai, toh aap hamari link se rasiid katne ki jankari le sakte hain.
