Darbhanga District Map & Block Records

Darbhanga ke sabhi 18 blocks (Sadar, Bahadurpur, Hayaghat, Hanuman Nagar, Jale, Keoti, Singhwara, Benipur, Baheri, Biraul, Ghanshyampur, Tardih, Alinagar, Kiratpur, Gora Bauram, Kusheshwar Asthan, Kusheshwar Asthan East) ka digital data hamari site par upalabdh hai.

1. Darbhanga Sadar: Land Record aur Circle Rate

Darbhanga Sadar mein zameen ki registry se pehle MVR (Minimum Value Register) check karna zaroori hai. Yahan naya survey tezi se chal raha hai aur Khatiyan ki digital copy upalabdh hai. Sadar block ke kisan LPC (Land Possession Certificate) ke liye online aavedan kar sakte hain.

2. Bahadurpur: Jamabandi aur Register 2 Details

Bahadurpur block mein Register 2 (Panji-II) ka digital record update ho gaya hai. Agar aapka Mutation (Dakhil-Kharij) reject ho gaya hai, toh aap case status check karke sudhaar karwa sakte hain. Yahan Schedule Batwara ke liye stamp paper ki jankari circle office se mil sakti hai.

3. Hayaghat: Zameen Registry aur MVR Rate

Hayaghat mein zameen ki keemat har saal badhti hai, isliye Circle Rate ki jankari hona anivarya hai. Purana Khatiyan aur Lagan Rasid online nikalne ke liye aapko thana number aur khata-khesra pata hona chahiye.

4. Hanuman Nagar: Survey aur Khatiyan Status

Hanuman Nagar block ke naye survey mein Vashavali (Family Tree) ka mahatva sabse zyada hai. Agar aapka parivaarik batwara (Panchnama) ho chuka hai, toh naye khatiyan mein apna naam chadhwane ke liye form bharein.

5. Keoti: Jamabandi Panji aur Mutation

Keoti block mein zameen ka Net-book update hona zaroori hai. Agar aapki rasid nahi kat rahi hai, toh iska matlab hai ki aapka Jamabandi digital record mein nahi chadhaya gaya hai.

6. Singhwara: Property Registry aur Documents

Singhwara block ke liye LPC Online apply karne ki suvidha ab live hai. Yahan ki zameen ke purane records (CS/RS) nikalne ke liye mauza wise search karein.

7. Jale: Batwara aur Stamp Paper Process

Jale block mein Schedule Batwara karte waqt stamp duty ka vishesh dhyan rakhein. Panchnama draft karne ke liye sabhi hissedaro ki sahmati anivarya hai.

8. Benipur: Circle Office aur Land Records

Benipur ek bada block hai jahan Register 2 ki nakal ke liye kafi bheed rehti hai. Hamari site par aap Khatiyan aur Jamabandi ki digital copy ke liye inquiry kar sakte hain.

Darbhanga ke Baaki Blocks (Biraul, Baheri, Ghanshyampur, etc.)

Darbhanga ke baaki blocks jaise Biraul, Baheri, Ghanshyampur, Tardih, Alinagar, Kiratpur, Gora Bauram, Kusheshwar Asthan, aur Kusheshwar Asthan East mein bhi land survey 2026 ka kaam pragati par hai. In sabhi blocks mein MVR Circle Rate aur LPC Online status check karne ke liye apne khata number ka upyog karein.

बिहार जमीन पर लोन और KCC की जानकारी

हमारे mithilanchal ke के किसान भाइयों,अगर आप अपनी जमीन पर बैंक से लोन या Kisan Credit Card (KCC) बनवाना चाहते हैं, तो आपको नीचे दिए गए दस्तावेजों की आवश्यकता होगी:

  • LPC (Land Possession Certificate): बिहार में लोन के लिए एलपीसी सबसे महत्वपूर्ण दस्तावेज है। LPC Apply Kaise Karein? (यहाँ देखें)
  • Adhyatan Jamabandi (Current Jamabandi): आपकी जमीन की रसीद अपडेटेड होनी चाहिए।
  • Khatiyan aur Vanshavali: वंशावली की जरूरत पुश्तैनी जमीन के मामले में पड़ती है।
