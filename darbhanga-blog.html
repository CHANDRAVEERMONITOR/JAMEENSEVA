2. Naya aur Purana Khatiyan Kaise Nikale?

Darbhanga mein abhi naya survey chal raha hai. Isliye aapko do tarah ke khatiyan ki zaroorat pad sakti hai:

  • Purana Khatiyan (Cadastral Survey): Ye aapke dada-pardada ke naam par hota hai. Isse "Vashavali" (Family Tree) prove hoti hai.
  • Naya Khatiyan (Special Survey): Bihar Sarkar ab naye digital records bana rahi hai jo online dekhe ja sakte hain.

3. Schedule Batwara aur Panchnama (Stamp Paper)

Zameen ka aapsi batwara sabse bada mudda hota hai. Agar aap Schedule Batwara ya Panchnama bina stamp paper ke karte hain, toh uski koi legal value nahi hoti.

  • Vashavali (Pedigree): Batwara se pehle sahi Vashavali banana zaroori hai.
  • Stamp Paper Value: Zameen ke sarkari rate (MVR) ke hisaab se stamp paper kharidna hota hai.
