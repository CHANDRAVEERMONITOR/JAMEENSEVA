Expert Analysis: Is Batwara Format ki Khasiyat

  • Stamp Paper Validity: Ye format ₹100 ke Non-Judicial stamp paper par likhne ke liye design kiya gaya hai[cite: 3, 4].
  • Hissedar ki Puri Jankari: Isme har hissedar ka naam, pita ka naam, umr aur Aadhar Number daalne ki jagah hai[cite: 18, 20, 21].
  • Detailed Land Table: Mauza, Thana No, Khata, Khesra aur Rakwa ka pura table diya gaya hai taaki koi galti na ho.
  • Chauhaddi (Boundaries): Har hisse ki charon dishao (Uttar, Dakshin, Purab, Pashchim) ki jankari shamil hai[cite: 55, 67, 81].
  • Vanshavali Connection: Isme purvajon se lekar abhi tak ke hissedaron ka Vanshvriksh (Family Tree) diya gaya hai.
  • Legal Declaration: Ye bina kisi dabav aur poori manasik swasthyata mein likha gaya ghoshna-patra hai[cite: 96].
  • Local Authority Validation: Sarpanch aur Gram Kachahari ke hastakshar aur mohar ki jagah di gayi hai[cite: 34, 101].
