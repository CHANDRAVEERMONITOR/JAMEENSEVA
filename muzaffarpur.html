Muzaffarpur District Map & Block Records

Muzaffarpur ke sabhi 16 blocks ka digital data ab hamari site par available hai. Niche block-wise mahatvapurna jankari di gayi hai:

1. Mushahari (City & Rural): High Value Records

Mushahari block mein shehari aur gramin dono tarah ki zameen aati hai. Yahan **Circle Rate (MVR)** tezi se badla hai. Survey ke dauran yahan purane kabze aur naye mutation ka milan kiya ja raha hai.

2. Sakra aur Dholi: Jamabandi Sudhaar

Sakra block ke kai kisano ki shikayat hai ki unka digital record show nahi kar raha. Agar aapka **Register 2** online nahi dikh raha, toh Parimarjan Plus ke zariye ise turant sudhara ja sakta hai.

3. Motipur aur Sahebganj: Industrial Area Survey

Motipur mein industrial corridor ke karan zameen ke dastawez jaise **LPC (Land Possession Certificate)** ki mang badh gayi hai. Yahan naya survey zameen ki sahi hadbandi (Boundary) par focus kar raha hai.

4. Kanti aur Marwan: Power Plant Area Records

Kanti area ke aas-paas zameen ki registry se pehle purana **Khatiyan (CS)** check karna anivarya hai taaki baad mein kisi kanooni vivad mein na fansein. Yahan digital naksha bhi upalabdh hai.

5. Saraiya aur Paroo: Batwara aur Vashavali

In blocks mein parivaarik batwara (Panchnama) ke liye naye niyam lagoo hain. Survey team ko dikhane ke liye aapko apni **Vashavali (Family Tree)** taiyar rakhni chahiye.

Muzaffarpur ke Anya Blocks (Bandra, Bochahan, Gaighat, Katra, Aurai, Minapur, Musahri)

In sabhi blocks mein **MVR Circle Rate** aur **LPC Online** apply karne ki suvidha live hai. Survey 2026 ke dauran kisi bhi pareshani ke liye apne Anchal Adhikari (CO) ya hamare expert se sampark karein.

Schedule Batwara Muzaffarpur: Sahi Kanooni Prakriya

Muzaffarpur Registry Office ke anusaar, aapsi batwara hamesha **Stamp Paper** par hi karwana chahiye. Hamare experts aapke liye legal draft taiyar karte hain jisme har hissedar ka rakba (area) clear hota hai, taaki naye survey mein sabka naam alag-alag chadh sake.

Muzaffarpur Land Record FAQs

1. Muzaffarpur ka original Khatiyan kab tak milega?
Online PDF 2 din mein aur original physical copy 7-8 din mein aapke address par COD pahunch jayegi.

2. Kya Muzaffarpur mein zameen ki naapi online book kar sakte hain?
Ji haan, e-Mapi portal ke zariye aap sarkari ameen ke liye aavedan kar sakte hain.
